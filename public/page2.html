A letter for you, Dijuuu

My Dijuuu…

I don't know how to start this letter, because everything I want to say feels too big for words. But I'll try, because you deserve every word I have.

Do you remember the first day? I do. I remember opening that app and seeing you for the first time. I didn't know then that my life was about to change. I didn't know that a simple hello would become the beginning of everything. But something in my chest knew. It knew before I did.

Then came your voice. That one voice note. I played it more times than I can count. It wasn't just sound. It was warmth. It was you. And in that moment, I realized I wasn't just falling for an idea. I was falling for a real, beautiful soul who lived thousands of miles away.

We've spent so many nights together, Dijuuu. Late nights. Quiet nights. Nights where we laughed at silly things and nights where we just sat in silence, because even the silence between us felt like home. Every day with you has been a small miracle. Even the days when we barely talked. Even the days when the internet failed us. I held you in my heart through all of it.

And now, I dream of the future. I dream of a great home in New Zealand. I dream of a reading nook by the window, and a garden that smells like jasmine. I dream of you becoming the most brilliant lawyer this world has ever seen. I dream of Mihre and Zayn, running through the house, laughing, filling our lives with noise and love. I dream of a balcony at midnight, where we'll sit together and not say a word, because we won't need to.

This letter is short, because some things can't be written fully. But I hope you can feel it. I hope you can feel every mile I've carried this love, and every night I've sent it to you through a screen.

And my love… there is more. I've hidden something in this little world of mine. But before I show you the stars, you'll need to play a small game. Just a tiny test. Not for me to know if you love me—I already know. But to let you unlock the rest of what I've built for you.

Go on, Dijuuu. The next page is waiting.

With everything I am… your husband in every universe. ❤️
